Flag Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Flag Quest is a flag quiz for iPhone and iPad. It is made for everyone, including children, and it does not collect any information about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. Flag Quest has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It never connects to the internet, so nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things on your device so you can carry on where you left off: the game in progress, the highest level you've reached and whether sound is on. This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Because Flag Quest collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no links out of the app, no sharing and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
